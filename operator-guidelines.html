Content Guidelines
& Compliance Rules

Every operator in the Silent Syndicate network must follow these guidelines. They exist to protect you legally, protect the network's reputation, and ensure we attract the right people — not the wrong ones.

// CRITICAL — READ FIRST
Never make health claims about Kangen water or Enagic products. The health and wellness claims sometimes associated with alkaline/ionised water are not strongly supported by mainstream scientific consensus. Making these claims in your content exposes you personally to ACCC scrutiny under Australian Consumer Law, and exposes the entire network to compliance risk. Market the income opportunity. Let Enagic's own official product materials handle product education.
// IMPORTANT — INCOME REPRESENTATIONS
Any income figure you show must be accompanied by the income disclaimer. This applies to screenshots, numbers in captions, testimonial results, and projections. The disclaimer is not optional. Under Australian Consumer Law, unqualified income claims in marketing constitute misleading conduct. Always add: "Results are not typical. Individual results vary significantly. Most participants earn less."

05
The Breakaway Mechanic — How to Discuss It

The breakaway mechanic is the most commonly misunderstood part of Enagic's structure. Here's how to explain it honestly when it comes up.

// WHAT IT IS
In Enagic's 8-point commission system, if a distributor in your downline achieves a higher rank than you, they begin capturing more commission points — including some that previously flowed to you. This is called the breakaway mechanic. It creates an incentive to keep building and stay active. It is not a hidden trap — it is a structural feature that rewards active operators and protects high performers.
✓ HOW TO EXPLAIN IT HONESTLY
"The Enagic rank system rewards people who stay active and keep building. If someone in your network outranks you, they capture more of the commission structure — which is exactly why the system keeps operators motivated long-term. It's not a set-and-forget model. The more you build, the more protected and profitable your position becomes."
