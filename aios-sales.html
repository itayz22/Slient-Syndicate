AUTOMATE
Recurring tasks are permanently removed from your plate. AIOS identifies what you do repeatedly, builds automation for it once, and runs it forever. This is the compound layer.

AIOS doesn't just manage your current system — it builds new assets, new funnels, new sequences, and new content based on what the data shows is working. The system grows itself.
