How It Works

Three steps to full visibility

AIOS connects to your Enagic account and funnel data automatically. No manual data entry, no spreadsheets.

01
Connect Your AC Account
Enter your Enagic AC number and API credentials once. AIOS authenticates with Enagic's system and your Netlify functions to establish secure data feeds for all seven dashboard modules.
02
Data Flows In
Every form submission, email open, chatbot session, and sale is captured in real time. The scoring engine processes each lead and updates pipeline stages automatically — no intervention required.
03
Monitor and Act
Log in each morning to your briefing. Drill into hot leads, review overnight sequences, track commission progress, and adjust your content strategy — all from one unified interface.
