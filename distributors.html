The Network Layer — Honest Explanation

When people in your network (distributors you recruited) make direct sales, you earn a commission override on each of their transactions. This is Enagic's upline structure — it exists in most direct-sales companies.

The math: If you have 3 operators in your network each making 2 sales/month at the Launcher tier, that's 6 additional commission events per month generating override income on top of your direct sales.

The honest part: Building a network takes time. It's a second-order effect, not a first-month outcome. Most operators earn primarily from direct sales for their first 3–6 months. Network overrides are a bonus, not the primary income model.
