Full Transparency

Here's Exactly What This Is.

// STRAIGHT TALK — NO FINE PRINT
Apex Network is a structured distributor network built on Enagic's direct sales platform. Enagic manufactures Kangen water ionisation machines — premium wellness products retailing between $3,000 and $5,000. When you become a distributor and bring in another distributor, you earn a commission directly from Enagic. That commission ranges from $2,400 to $6,330 depending on the tier you and your recruit joined at.

There is also a network layer. When distributors in your downline make sales, you earn override commissions on their volume. The deeper your network, the more it compounds — without you being involved in every transaction.

What Apex Network adds on top of Enagic's model is an AI-automated funnel system — so instead of cold-calling, door-knocking, or pitching to people you know, you run a content-driven digital funnel that attracts and qualifies leads automatically.

Here's How It Works.

// HONEST BREAKDOWN
This is a distributor network. We're not going to dress it up.
When you join and bring in other distributors, Enagic pays you an override commission on their sales. That's the network layer. It's not a secret — it's how the compensation structure is designed, and it's the same model used by major direct sales companies globally.

What makes this different from traditional network marketing is what you're not doing. You're not cold-messaging strangers. You're not selling to family. You're not buying bulk inventory to resell.
