Real Questions, Straight Answers

"Is this an MLM?"

Enagic is a direct-sales company, not a pyramid scheme. Your primary income is from direct sales ($2,400–$6,330 per transaction), not from recruiting. The team override structure exists but isn't required to earn well.

"The investment is too high."

Break-even at the Launcher tier is ~4 sales ($15,000 ÷ $4,300 = 3.5 sales). If you can't make 4 sales in 90 days, this isn't the right vehicle. If you can, you're profitable by month 3.

"Do I have to recruit people?"

No. You earn your full commission on every direct sale regardless of whether your buyers become distributors. Building a network accelerates income but isn't required.

"I don't have an audience."

The funnel is designed for cold traffic. TikTok's algorithm reaches non-followers organically. You don't need existing followers — you need compelling content and a working funnel.
