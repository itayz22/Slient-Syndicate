Chatbot Script

Complete conversation flows, objection handling responses, and qualifying question sequences used by the Silent Syndicate AI chatbot. The chatbot runs fully client-side with no API calls.

WELCOME FLOW — INITIAL GREETING
TRIGGER: Page load after 3 seconds, or first user message
AI
Hey. I'm the Silent Syndicate AI — I handle all enquiries for this operation.

Before you read the page, tell me: what actually brought you here? Are you looking to make money online, or you're specifically interested in the distributor model?
OBJECTION HANDLING FLOWS
TRIGGER KEYWORDS: "mlm", "pyramid", "scam", "scheme", "cult"
U
Is this an MLM / pyramid scheme?
AI
Fair question. Let me be direct.

Enagic is a direct sales company — not MLM in the traditional sense. You earn commissions on your own sales only. There's no recruitment requirement, no downline dependency, no "buy-in" to access commissions.

The 8-tier commission structure means some distributors above you earn overrides when you sell — that's the closest thing to a "network" element. But your income comes from your sales, full stop.

If you want, I can walk you through the exact pay structure with numbers?
TRIGGER KEYWORDS: "cost", "price", "expensive", "afford", "investment", "how much"
U
How much does it cost to join?
AI
Three entry tiers:

• Striker — $2,400 investment, earn $2,400 per sale
• Launcher — $15,000 investment, earn $4,300 per sale
• Nuke — $20,000 investment, earn $6,330 per sale

Break-even at Launcher: 3.5 sales. At 1 sale per month that's about 4 months. At 2 sales per month, under 2 months.

This is real capital at risk. If that number feels uncomfortable, this probably isn't the right fit right now — and that's completely fine. What's your situation?
TRIGGER KEYWORDS: "legit", "real", "proof", "evidence", "fake", "trust"
U
Is this actually legit?
AI
Enagic has been operating since 1974. Headquartered in Osaka, Japan. They manufacture water ionisers sold in 190+ countries. The company itself is legitimate and well-documented.

What's less certain — for any operator — is your results. Income depends entirely on your content output and conversion rate. I won't pretend otherwise.

The operators in this system are generating real income, but it took consistent daily content for 4–8 weeks before meaningful lead volume started. Anyone promising faster than that is overstating it.
TRIGGER KEYWORDS: "time", "busy", "hours", "part time", "full time", "passive"
U
How much time does it take?
AI
Content creation: 30–60 minutes per day for one short-form video.

The funnel, chatbot, email sequences — fully automated. You don't handle leads manually. The AI qualifies them, scores them, routes them. You review your dashboard, respond to HOT leads only.

Realistic active time once the system is set up: 1–2 hours per day. Mostly content. Not passive, but not a full-time job either.

Is that kind of time commitment something you can commit to consistently for 60 days?
QUALIFYING QUESTION SEQUENCE

The chatbot routes users toward the application form after gathering qualification signals. These questions feed into the score engine.

TRIGGER: User expresses interest in joining / starting
AI
Okay. Three quick questions — just to understand if this is a realistic fit for you right now.

First: do you have the capital available? Striker entry is $2,400, Launcher is $15,000. Not asking you to commit — just whether it's accessible without hardship.
U
Yes, I have capital ready.
AI
Good. Second: timeline. Are you looking to start this month, or are you in research mode?
U
Ready to start now.
AI
Last one: content creation. Can you commit to posting one 60-second video per day for the next 60 days — even if your first ones aren't great?
U
Yes, I can do that.
AI
You sound like a fit. Fill in the application — takes 3 minutes. The system scores your profile immediately and I'll route you to the right next step.

→ Apply now
POST-APPLICATION ROUTING LOGIC
HOT ≥70
Redirect immediately to page4-offer.html. Tag added in ActiveCampaign: SS-HOT. HOT automation triggers: 3-email urgent sequence within 24 hours. Owner notified by email.
WARM 40–69
Redirect to custom warm message on apply page. Tag: SS-WARM. Warm nurture automation triggers: 30-day 5-email sequence. Encouraged to revisit when capital situation improves.
COLD <40
Shown a polite "not a fit right now" message. Offered the free blueprint instead. Tag: none (or custom cold tag). No automation triggered. Contact stored for future remarketing.
