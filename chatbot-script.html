Flow 01
The Opening Conversation.

Triggers after 30 seconds on any page. Warm, not pushy. The goal here is to open a conversation — not to sell.

// AI — OPENING MESSAGE
Hey 👋 — I saw you checking out the system. Got any questions? I'm happy to break down how it works, what the numbers look like, or what happens after you join.
USER RESPONDS
// USER — Common opening: "How does it work?"
How does it actually work?
// AI — SYSTEM EXPLANATION (keep concise)
Good question. At a high level — it's a 4-stage AI funnel connected to a high-ticket income model called Enagic.

Stage 1: AI-powered content attracts people into your funnel.
Stage 2: An automated 7-day email sequence educates and builds belief — no manual work.
Stage 3: An application form qualifies serious buyers automatically.
Stage 4: Qualified leads land on an offer page and join directly. No calls needed.

When someone joins at the Launcher tier, you earn $4,300 in commission. The AI handles the whole process. Want me to go deeper on any part of that?
Flow 02
The Qualification Conversation.

When someone seems genuinely interested, the AI gently qualifies them. The goal is to identify HOT leads — not to pressure anyone.

// USER — Shows interest
This actually sounds interesting. I've been looking for something like this.
// AI — QUALIFY QUESTION 1: Situation
Nice — what's your current situation? Like are you working full-time, running something on the side, or somewhere in between? Just helps me point you toward what's most relevant.
// USER — Typical answer
I work full time but I'm trying to find something I can build on the side.
// AI — QUALIFY QUESTION 2: Goal
That's actually the perfect setup for this — the system runs in the background once it's built, so it doesn't need constant attention. What kind of income would actually change things for you? Like is there a number in your head?
// USER — Income goal
Honestly if I could replace my salary — about $8–10k a month — that would change everything.
// AI — QUALIFY QUESTION 3: Investment readiness
$8–10K is very achievable with this model — that's 2–3 sales per month at the Launcher tier.

One thing worth knowing upfront: there is an investment to get started, similar to buying a franchise licence. The tiers range from $8,500 to $20,000 depending on the commission level you want. Is that something you'd be in a position to consider if the opportunity made sense?
✓ YES PATH
User says yes / open to it:

"Great — that's actually the most important qualifier. People who are ready to invest in themselves are the ones who get results fastest. I'd recommend going straight to the application — it takes 3 minutes and your offer unlocks immediately after. Want me to send you the link?"
✗ NOT YET PATH
User says not right now / hesitant:

"Totally fair — it's a real investment and you should only do it when it makes sense. The free blueprint has the full breakdown of how the numbers work, so you can see exactly what the ROI looks like before making any decision. Have you grabbed that yet?"
Objection Library
Every Pushback.
Exactly How To Respond.

These are the 12 most common objections. The AI is trained on all of these. Click each to see the exact response.

"Is this an MLM?"
+
// AI RESPONSE
It's a fair question and I'd rather answer it straight than dodge it. The Enagic model does have a network structure — when someone you bring in makes a sale, you earn an override commission on top of your own sales. So yes, there's a network element.

Where it's different from traditional MLM: the income comes primarily from your own direct sales, not from recruiting. The product — Kangen water machines — is a real, legitimate product that Enagic has been selling globally for 40+ years. You're not buying inventory to resell to friends and family. The AI funnel finds buyers automatically.

If you want the full breakdown of how the commission structure works, it's in the free blueprint. Does that help clarify things?
"Is this too good to be true?"
+
// AI RESPONSE
I get why it sounds that way — high commissions, automated system, no cold calls. But let me break it down concretely.

The commissions are high because the product investment is high — $8,500 to $20,000 per buyer. Enagic is a premium product sold globally since 1974. That's what creates a $2,400–$6,330 commission per sale — it's not magic, it's math.

The automation is real — it's a funnel built on standard tools like ActiveCampaign and an AI chatbot. Nothing exotic.

What it isn't: passive income from day one. You have to build the funnel, create content to drive traffic, and give it 30–60 days to work. The people who get results do the setup work. The people who don't are hoping the system does everything with zero input.
"I don't have any tech skills."
+
// AI RESPONSE
You genuinely don't need any. The whole system is built on tools with no-code interfaces — Netlify for hosting, ActiveCampaign for emails, Typeform for the application form. Everything has step-by-step setup docs.

Most operators are live within 48 hours of getting access. If you can use a smartphone and follow instructions, you can set this up. The tech stack guide walks through every single step with exact screenshots. What part are you most worried about?
"I don't have an audience or followers."
+
// AI RESPONSE
Good — you don't need one, and honestly an existing audience can sometimes work against you because you're trying to sell something different to people who know you for something else.

The traffic strategy for this system is based on new content attracting new people — mostly TikTok and Instagram Reels targeting people who are already looking for income opportunities. You're not selling to your existing network. The content finds strangers who are already in the right mindset, and the funnel converts them automatically.
"How long before I make my first sale?"
+
// AI RESPONSE
Honest answer: most people who do the setup properly and post content consistently see their first HOT lead in weeks 2–3, and their first confirmed sale in weeks 3–5.

The variables that speed this up: how much content you post (volume matters more than quality early on), whether you're on TikTok where organic reach is highest, and how quickly your funnel is set up.

The variable that slows it down: doing the setup but not posting content. The funnel doesn't fill itself — you still need to drive traffic into it. Once traffic is flowing, the automation handles everything else.
"The investment is too high."
+
// AI RESPONSE
I hear you — it's a real number. Here's how I'd think about it: at the Launcher tier ($15,000 investment), your commission per sale is $4,300. Three sales and you've recovered your full investment. Four sales and you're profitable.

Compare that to buying a traditional franchise — most cost $50,000–$500,000 and take years to break even. This is a significantly lower barrier with a significantly faster break-even.

That said — if the investment genuinely isn't accessible right now, don't do it. The people who get results are the ones who invest with money they can afford to put to work, not money they can't afford to lose. Is the number the issue, or is it more about confidence in the model?
"I've tried things like this before and they didn't work."
+
// AI RESPONSE
That's actually one of the most valuable things you can tell me — what have you tried? Knowing what didn't work helps figure out whether this is actually different or just more of the same.

The most common failure modes I hear: low-ticket products that can't create real income, models that require a huge following you don't have, or systems that depend on you manually selling to people you know. This model is deliberately designed around none of those things — high-ticket commissions, cold traffic funnels, and AI automation instead of personal selling.

But if you've tried high-ticket before and it didn't work — that's worth unpacking. What happened?
"Do I have to recruit people?"
+
// AI RESPONSE
No — and this is an important distinction. You're not recruiting people into a scheme. You're attracting buyers for a real product.

The people who enter your funnel are looking for an income opportunity — they opt in voluntarily, go through an education sequence, apply of their own accord, and make a purchase decision independently. Your AI funnel finds and converts them. You're not going through your contact list or DMing strangers.

Yes, the people who buy become Enagic distributors themselves — but that's a feature, not the core pitch. Your income comes from them purchasing the product, not from them recruiting others.
"What support do I get after joining?"
+
// AI RESPONSE
When you join, you get: immediate access to the AIOS dashboard, all funnel page assets, the 7-day email sequence templates, the tech setup guide, and the full traffic strategy. Everything is self-guided and documented.

You also get access to the Apex Network operator community — active people running the same system who can answer questions, share what's working, and help troubleshoot. Most operators are live within 48 hours with the setup guide.

The support model is designed to be low-touch — the system is built so you don't need hand-holding. But the community is there if you need it. Is there something specific you're worried about getting stuck on?
"Can I see proof this works?"
+
// AI RESPONSE
Fair and important question. The results page has real operator stories — Sarah K. in Brisbane at $14,200/month by month 3, James R. in Melbourne with his first sale at Day 19, Michelle T. in Perth with a $6,100 first commission.

I'd also point you to the income disclaimer — we're transparent that results vary significantly and most people don't hit those numbers. Those are real people doing well. There are also people who join and don't take consistent action and see no results.

The model works when you work it. The math is sound, the tools are real, and the commissions are paid by Enagic — a 40-year-old company. Want me to share any specific numbers or show you how the ROI calculator works?
"I need to think about it / talk to my partner."
+
// AI RESPONSE
That's completely reasonable — this is a real investment and it should be a considered decision. Don't let anyone pressure you into it.

What I'd suggest: grab the free blueprint if you haven't already — it has the full system breakdown, the commission structure, and the ROI calculator. That gives you and your partner everything you need to evaluate it properly without any sales pressure.

Is there anything specific I can answer now that would help make that conversation easier?
"Do I need to sell the water machines personally?"
+
// AI RESPONSE
No — and this is one of the things that makes this model different. The buyers come to you through the AI funnel. They've already been educated on the opportunity, they've applied, and they arrive at the offer page ready to make a decision. You're not cold-selling machines to anyone.

The machine is part of the package — when someone joins at a given tier, the Kangen machine is included in what they receive. They're buying into the system and the machine comes with it. You're not a door-to-door salesperson. You're an operator running an automated pipeline.
