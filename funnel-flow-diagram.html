// STAGE 03
QUALIFY
FILTER + SCORE
// What Happens
Not everyone who opts in should buy. The qualification stage protects your time and your ecosystem. An application form asks 7 key questions about situation, goals, budget readiness, and intent.

Responses are automatically scored. High-intent applicants get fast-tracked to the offer or a call. Low-intent leads stay in a nurture sequence. You only ever deal with people who are genuinely ready.
1
Apply CTA Triggered

Email sequence or chatbot sends them to the application page

2
Form Completed

7-question application captures intent, budget, situation

3
Auto-Score Applied

Zapier + scoring logic tags contacts as Hot / Warm / Not Yet

4
Routing Fires

Hot leads → offer page. Warm → nurture. Not Yet → long-term sequence.
