HeyGen Setup Guide

Up and Running
in Under 20 Minutes.

Follow these steps once. After setup, creating each video takes under 10 minutes.

1
Sign up for HeyGen Creator ($29/month)
Go to heygen.com → Sign up → Choose Creator plan ($29/month or $24/month annual). Start with monthly — upgrade to annual after your first commission.
2
Create your AI Avatar (Photo-to-Video)
Dashboard → Avatars → Create Avatar → Photo Avatar (Avatar IV). Upload one clear headshot — good lighting, neutral background, facing forward. HeyGen animates it into a talking avatar. This is your permanent avatar for all videos. Takes ~5 minutes to process.
3
Set your voice (Optional: clone yours)
Either: select a stock voice from HeyGen's library (700+ options — pick one that sounds like a confident Australian professional), OR sign up to ElevenLabs ($5/month), record 30 seconds of yourself speaking, clone your voice, and use ElevenLabs to generate the voiceover audio — then upload to HeyGen instead of using their voices.
4
Set video format to 9:16 vertical
In HeyGen AI Studio → New Video → select 9:16 Portrait format. This is the TikTok/Reels format. Every video should be this ratio. Set 1080p quality. Background: solid dark colour or blurred office/minimal backdrop works best.
5
Generate your first test video
Paste the first script from above (Pain — "The side hustle trap"). Select your avatar. Select your voice. Hit Generate. Watch it render in ~3 minutes. Download and review. This is what your content looks like. Adjust avatar or voice if needed.
6
Import to CapCut and add captions
Open CapCut (free, web or app). Import your HeyGen video → Text → Auto Captions → Generate. Style: white bold text, black outline, position at lower third. Add a subtle background track (CapCut's library → Commercial Music → pick something calm at 8–10% volume). Export 1080p.
7
Schedule via Later and post
Sign up to later.com (free). Connect your TikTok and Instagram accounts. Upload your video. Write your caption (template below). Schedule for 7pm AEST TikTok, 6pm AEST Instagram. Done.
