What To Post About
Four Content Pillars.

Every piece of content you make fits into one of these four categories. Rotate between them. Never stay on one topic too long.

// PILLAR 01
PAIN & PROBLEM
Content that names the frustration your audience feels. Trading time for money. The content treadmill. Being stuck. This creates pattern interrupts and stops the scroll.
"Why posting every day is making you broke"
"The income model nobody talks about"
"Why your side hustle has a ceiling"
// PILLAR 02
BELIEF SHIFT
Content that challenges assumptions and opens minds to a different way of thinking about income. Not a pitch — just a perspective shift that makes people think differently.
"The quiet operators are always winning"
"What a $6,000 commission feels like"
"Why I stopped creating content and started building systems"
// PILLAR 03
PROOF & RESULTS
Real numbers, real timelines, real outcomes. Commission screenshots, pipeline screenshots, operator stories. People need to see it's real before they'll believe it's possible for them.
"Week 3 update — first HOT lead converted"
"How Sarah made $14,200 in month 3"
"What my pipeline looked like in month 1 vs month 4"
// PILLAR 04
EDUCATION & SYSTEM
Content that explains how the system works — the AI funnel, the 4 stages, the callless model, the AIOS. This is your most shareable content and attracts the most serious leads.
"I explained the AI funnel in 60 seconds"
"How a lead goes from stranger to $4,300 commission"
"The 5 layers of the AIOS — quick breakdown"

The Launch Ramp
Your First 30 Days
Of Traffic.

Week by week. What to focus on, what to post, what target to hit.

WEEK 1 — Days 1–7
// FOUNDATION
Target: 50 opt-ins

What to post

3x Pain/Problem content — name the frustrations your audience feels
2x Belief shift — "quiet operators", "systems vs hustle"
1x Education — explain the funnel simply in 60 seconds

Focus on

Consistency over perfection — post imperfectly and improve
Watch your analytics — what's getting saves and shares?
Link in bio pointing to page1-optin.html
WEEK 2 — Days 8–14
// DOUBLE DOWN
Target: 120 opt-ins total

What to post

Replicate whatever got the most saves/shares from Week 1
2x Proof content — screenshot pipeline, share a number
Start adding Facebook — repurpose your best TikToks

Focus on

First leads entering your email sequence — watch open rates
Any chatbot conversations — what questions are people asking?
Refine your bio and CTA based on what's working
WEEK 3 — Days 15–21
// FIRST APPLICATIONS
Target: 220 opt-ins total

What to post

Push Education content harder — the AIOS, the 5 layers, the system
Use chatbot questions as content ideas — answer them publicly
1x transparent "Day X update" showing your pipeline or numbers

Focus on

First HOT leads should be arriving in Stage 3 by now
Check Zapier — are HOT leads getting routed to offer page?
First potential commission within reach by end of week
WEEK 4 — Days 22–30
// FIRST SALE
Target: First commission

What to post

Post your first result — even a pipeline screenshot is powerful
More proof, more system education, more belief content
Start making content about what happens AFTER joining

Focus on

Review your email open rates — what's getting clicks?
Scale whatever content format is driving the most opt-ins
Start planning paid ads with any first commission
// THE ONE RULE
Volume Beats Perfection.
Every Time.
The operators who get results fastest are not the ones who make the best content. They're the ones who make the most content. Post before you're ready. Improve as you go. The algorithm rewards frequency and punishes waiting.
