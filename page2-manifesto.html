CHAPTER 03

How It Works

01
ATTRACTION — Drive traffic to the opt-in page

Short-form content on TikTok and Instagram (60-second reels) drives cold traffic. The hook: transparent, numbers-first, no hype. Honest attracts serious people. Opt-in gate: free AI Income Blueprint. Collected: first name + email.

02
EDUCATION — 7-day email sequence

ActiveCampaign automation delivers 7 emails over 7 days. Day 0: blueprint + full disclosure. Days 1–6: Enagic model, the funnel, the product, social proof, objections, mindset. Day 7: application CTA. Designed to qualify and filter, not just sell.

03
QUALIFICATION — Application + AI scoring

7-field application form scores leads automatically. HOT (investment ready) → offer page. WARM (close but unsure) → extended nurture. COLD (not capital-ready) → back to education. The chatbot handles real-time objections on the offer page.

04
CONVERSION — Tiered offer + Enagic registration

Three tier options with live ROI calculator. Leads pre-educated on investment before they reach this page. Conversion happens via Enagic's official registration process. You earn your commission when the payment clears.
