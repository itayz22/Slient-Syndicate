02 — CONTENT ENGINE
THE 5-PILLAR
CONTENT FORMULA

Rotate through these 5 pillars. Post once daily. Each piece drives to your opt-in link. One great video can generate 10+ leads.

// THE FRAME
Hook (3 sec) → Problem (10 sec) → System (20 sec) → Proof (15 sec) → CTA (5 sec)
Total: 60–90 seconds. Never longer.
PILLAR 01
PAIN → POSSIBILITY
Start with the problem (underpaid, trapped, no exit). Pivot to the system that changed it.
"I used to [problem]. Then I found a system that pays $225–$2,595 per sale..."
PILLAR 02
THE MECHANISM
Explain HOW it works. Enagic model, tiered commissions, no ceiling. No hype — just the structure.
"Here's exactly how I get paid $1,322 from a single customer..."
PILLAR 03
THE NUMBERS
Show the math. 3 sales/month at different tiers. What that means annually. Let the numbers speak.
"3 K8 Pro sales per month = $3,966. That's $47,592/year from 3 customers..."
PILLAR 04
WHO THIS IS FOR
Velvet rope. Name the person: wants income replacement, has $2.5K–$18K to invest, coachable.
"This is NOT for everyone. If you want to earn $500 for referring a friend, this isn't it..."
PILLAR 05
DAY IN THE LIFE
Show your actual routine. Morning briefing, content creation, lead pipeline. Aspirational reality.
"My Monday: check AC dashboard, 1 new HOT lead, post 1 video, done by noon..."

05 — NETWORK
RECRUITING YOUR
FIRST OPERATOR

Your first recruit is the most important. They prove the system works to you — and to everyone watching. Follow this process exactly.

STEP 01
Identify the Right Person
Someone with capital available, who wants income replacement, and is coachable. Not someone looking for a "side hustle." They need to be serious about building.
STEP 02
Send Them Into the Funnel
Your apex-index.html → apex-page2.html → apex-apply.html sequence does the selling. Don't present manually. Let the funnel qualify them. Your job is to answer questions after they've read it.
STEP 03
Follow Up on Application
If they score HOT, use the First Contact script. Walk them through apex-offer.html on a screen share if needed. Address objections with facts — not pressure.

Get Them Registered
Send apex-join.html?sponsorid=YOUR_ID. They register through Enagic using your Distributor ID. Once registered they're in your downline.
STEP 05
Onboard Them With This Page
Fork the repo. Replace sponsor IDs with theirs. Point them at their own Operator System page. Their Week 1 checklist starts now. You are their upline — support them through it.
STEP 06
Plug Into Agent OS
Show them apex-agent-os.html. Walk them through the Morning Briefing and Script Generator modules. The system builds their content engine from day one.
