// VIDEO SCRIPT FORMULA — 45 SECONDS
00–05s
HOOK — Lead with the number or contrast
Pattern interrupt first. A number, comparison, or counterintuitive statement. Never introduce yourself before the hook.
"I invested $11,000 into a distribution network. That single investment generates up to $1,322 every time someone on my team makes a sale."
05–15s
CONTEXT — Who you are and why the contrast matters
One sentence. Your day job + what you built on the side. Makes it relatable to the target audience.
"I'm a full-time painter on the Sunshine Coast. I'm not quitting my job yet — but I built something that runs in parallel."
15–30s
MECHANISM — How the system works in plain terms
Two or three sentences. AI content → funnel → application → commission. Platform framing, never MLM language.
"AI generates the content. The content drives people to a funnel. The funnel filters serious operators. When operators make sales, I earn overrides."
30–40s
FILTER — It's not for everyone
Create exclusivity. Removes low-intent leads before they hit the funnel. Makes the right person lean in harder.
"This requires a real investment and a genuine commitment. If you're looking for a shortcut, this isn't it."
40–45s
CTA — One instruction only
Tell them one thing to do. Nothing else.
"Full breakdown is in the link in my bio."
