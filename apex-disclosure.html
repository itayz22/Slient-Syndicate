Purpose of This Statement

Apex Network is committed to complete transparency about income potential. This statement provides full disclosure of the commission structure, realistic income expectations, break-even timelines, and the factors that determine outcomes. It applies to all marketing content produced under the Apex Network brand.

// READ BEFORE PROCEEDING

The vast majority of Enagic distributors earn little or no income from their distributorship. Commission income is entirely dependent on product sales. There is no passive income, no guaranteed income, and no income from simply recruiting others. This is not a get-rich-quick opportunity. It requires consistent sales effort over an extended period.

The Commission Structure

Apex Network operates on Enagic's authorised distributor commission framework. All figures below are in Australian Dollars (AUD) including GST, based on Enagic's published compensation plan as of 2025. Commission rates and product prices are subject to change by Enagic International at any time without notice.

// HOW ENAGIC COMMISSIONS WORK

When a distributor makes a sale, Enagic pays a fixed commission per "point" in the transaction. The number of points — and therefore the commission per sale — is determined by the distributor's rank. Rank is determined by the total number of machines sold, not by time in the business.

Each product sale generates one or more "8-points" (one per machine in the transaction). The commission per point increases with rank. The commission figures below represent the total payment per complete transaction at each rank.

// 04
Realistic Month 1 Expectations

The following represents a realistic, honest breakdown of what a new operator can typically expect in their first month. This is not a projection of your results — it is provided to set accurate expectations.

WEEK 1
Setup & orientation
Enagic application submitted and being processed. Distributor ID not yet issued. Machine not yet received. No commissions can be earned during this period.
Commission earned: $0
WEEK 2
Machine arrives — learning begins
Machine delivered. Distributor ID typically issued. Account activation with Enagic. Learning the product, platform, and content system. Most new operators begin their first content attempts or outreach this week.
Commission earned: $0 — $365
WEEK 3–4
First outreach & potential first sale
Most operators are still building their process, content, and lead flow. A first sale in month 1 is possible but not typical. Operators who made warm contacts before registering have the best chance of a month 1 commission.
Commission earned: $0 — $365 (Entry) / $0 — $1,322 (Pro) / $0 — $2,595 (Elite)
// TYPICAL MONTH 1
$0 – $365
Most new operators make zero to one sale in their first month. Revenue from content and organic outreach typically builds over months 2–6. Month 1 is primarily a setup and learning phase.
// EXCEPTIONAL MONTH 1
$1,322 – $2,595+
Operators with a warm existing network, prior sales experience, or exceptional content reach can achieve multiple sales in month 1. This is not typical and should not be used as a planning assumption.

The honest baseline: Plan for $0 commission income in your first month. Any income in month 1 should be treated as a bonus. Investment recovery should be planned over a 6–18 month horizon depending on sales activity and tier.

// 05
Network Income — What It Requires

The "network math" figures referenced in Apex Network marketing (e.g. $10,309/month from 7 operators × 3 sales/month) represent a illustrative projection based on a specific scenario, not a typical or expected outcome. Below is the full breakdown and what it actually requires to achieve it.

// THE SCENARIO ILLUSTRATED

7 active operators each making 3 sales per month, all at Pro (Trifecta) tier, all maintaining 2A rank. Direct commissions ~$2,644/month + override income ~$7,665/month = ~$10,309/month total.

To reach this scenario requires:

01
Recruiting and onboarding 7 operators who each purchase a Trifecta or higher package (~$11,682 each)
02
Each of those 7 operators independently generating 3 Trifecta-equivalent sales per month from their own efforts
03
Maintaining your own personal sales to qualify for override commissions at the applicable rank
04
All operators remaining consistently active — inactive operators generate no commissions

There is no guarantee that recruited operators will be active, will make consistent sales, or will remain in the network. Override income depends entirely on the independent sales activity of your network — it cannot be controlled or guaranteed.

// 06
Factors That Determine Results

Income results within the Apex Network are determined by the following factors, none of which Apex Network or the network operator controls:

→
Individual sales activity — the number of qualified prospects you contact and convert each month
→
Sales skills and experience — ability to communicate value, handle objections, and close high-ticket sales
→
Market conditions — demand for the product in your specific market and network
→
Network activity — whether operators you recruit remain active and productive
→
Time investment — hours dedicated weekly to content creation, outreach, and follow-up
→
Enagic International decisions — changes to commission rates, product pricing, policies, or the compensation plan by Enagic
→
Consumer protection environment — regulatory changes affecting the direct sales or wellness product industry in Australia
