The 8-Point Commission System

When an Enagic machine is sold, the commission is distributed across up to 8 people in the distributor chain. Each "point" has a fixed dollar value. Your rank determines how many points you capture — both from your own sales and from your downline's sales.

How The Points Work

Every machine sale generates a fixed pool of commission — approximately $340–$350 per point × 8 points = ~$2,720–$2,800 total distributed. That pool gets split across the distributor chain based on rank.

A 1A distributor (entry rank) captures 1 point from their own sale. A 6A distributor (Launcher tier) captures 6 points. The remaining points flow to higher-ranked distributors further up the chain.

The higher your rank, the more of the available pool you capture — and the more you also capture from every sale your downline makes.

// THE BREAKAWAY MECHANIC — EXPLAINED PLAINLY
What Happens When Your Downline Outranks You
In Enagic's commission structure, rank determines point capture. If a distributor in your downline achieves a higher rank than you, they begin capturing more of the available commission points per sale — including some that previously flowed to you.

This is called the breakaway mechanic. It is not a hidden trap. It is a structural feature designed to reward active operators and create sustained motivation to build.
What it means in practice: If you join at Launcher (6A) and your recruit eventually reaches 6A themselves, they capture a similar commission structure to yours on their sales. Your override on their sales adjusts accordingly.
Why this is actually good: It ensures the system rewards performance at every level — not just seniority. Strong operators at every level are compensated for their own work.
How to protect your position: Stay active. Keep building your own sales volume and network. Operators who stay at the top of their own network in terms of rank and activity earn the most long-term.
The Apex Network angle: The AI funnel keeps you consistently generating new sales and new operators — which is exactly what protects and grows your rank position over time.
