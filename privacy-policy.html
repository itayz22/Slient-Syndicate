We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit the opt-in form on our landing page (first name, email address)

We use your personal information to:

We will never sell your personal information to third parties. We will never use your information for purposes unrelated to Silent Syndicate.

By submitting your email address on our website, you consent to receiving our email sequence and related marketing communications. Every email we send includes a clear, functional unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time by clicking unsubscribe or emailing us directly.

We use ActiveCampaign to manage our email list. ActiveCampaign stores your contact data on servers that comply with GDPR and relevant international privacy standards.

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:
