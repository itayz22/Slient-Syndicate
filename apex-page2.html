// The Mechanism
HOW APEX GENERATES COMMISSIONS

Apex Network sits on top of Enagic's 50-year authorised distribution framework. Enagic manufactures high-performance water ionisation systems retailing at $2,475–$18,469. They pay direct commissions to authorised distributors — up to $2,595 per sale, deposited 7–14 business days post-registration. We are those distributors. Our operators are the field.

01
AI Content System Drives Traffic
HeyGen AI avatar generates your video content from scripts. CapCut edits it. Later schedules it across TikTok and Instagram. The content runs the transparency formula: Problem → Mechanism → Proof → CTA. No filming. No editing. Content goes out daily.
02
Funnel Qualifies and Captures Leads
Traffic hits the Apex front door. Warm leads opt in and enter the nurture sequence. Hot leads apply directly. The funnel scores applicants (HOT/WARM/COLD) and routes them accordingly — no manual sorting required.
03
Operator Converts the Qualified Lead
You handle the final conversation with pre-qualified, investment-ready prospects. Close the sale. The buyer registers directly through apex-join.html — their Sponsor ID is pre-confirmed to your distributor number. Enagic processes the paperwork and pays the commission.
04
Network Commissions Compound Monthly
Every operator you recruit also earns commissions — and you earn override commissions on their sales. 7 active operators each making 3 sales per month = $9,625+ combined income from direct sales and overrides. The network floor grows every month.

// Why This Works
THE UNFAIR ADVANTAGES

Most distribution networks fail because the product is weak, the margin is thin, or the operator is left alone with no system. Apex Network is built to solve all three.

The product: Enagic water ionisers are real, premium, 50-year-old technology. Customers buy for health reasons and keep the product for years. There is no recurring subscription to cancel and no inventory to hold. The distributor is not selling to their friends — they're generating inbound interest through content.

The margin: $225–$2,595 per sale on a single transaction. No revenue sharing, no percentage cuts, no hidden deductions. The commission is the revenue. High-ticket, single-transaction, direct-to-bank.

The system: AI content generation means operators don't need to be on camera, skilled in editing, or experienced in social media. The transparency formula drives inbound curiosity at scale. The Apex funnel handles qualification. The operator handles conversion.
