01
// PHASE ONE — Do This First
Get Your Pages Live Online
~2 hours
🌐Netlify
netlify.com → FREE to host
Netlify hosts your HTML files and gives them a live URL that works on any phone or browser. It takes about 5 minutes. You can use a free yoursite.netlify.app URL to start, then connect your own domain later.
// SETUP STEPS
1
Create a free account
Go to netlify.com, sign up with email or Google. No credit card needed.
2
Drag and drop your files
On the Netlify dashboard, find the "Deploy manually" area. Drag ALL your HTML files in at once — page1-optin, page2-manifesto, page3-apply, page4-offer, aios-dashboard, aios-sales.
3
Get your live URL
Netlify generates a URL instantly — something like purple-syndicate-7f2a.netlify.app. This is your live funnel.
4
(Optional) Connect your own domain
Buy silentsyndicateAU.com from Namecheap (~$12/yr). In Netlify → Domain Settings → Add custom domain. Follow the DNS instructions. Takes 24h to propagate.
02
// PHASE TWO — The Email Engine
Connect Your Email Platform
~4 hours
📧ActiveCampaign
activecampaign.com → $29/month
This is the brain of the funnel. It stores every lead, sends your 7-day email sequence automatically, applies tags based on behaviour, and tells you exactly who opened what. Everything runs through here.
// SETUP STEPS
1
Create your account
Sign up at activecampaign.com. Choose the Lite plan at $29/month — it covers everything you need at this stage.
2
Create your list
In ActiveCampaign → Lists → New List. Call it "Silent Syndicate Leads". This is where all opt-ins land.
3
Create your form embed
In ActiveCampaign → Forms → New Form. Create an inline form with Name + Email fields. Copy the embed code — you'll replace the form on page1-optin.html with this code.
4
Build your automation
Go to Automations → New Automation → "Start from scratch". Set the trigger as "Subscribes to list". This is where your 7-day sequence will live — we'll add the emails here once they're written.
5
Set up your tags
Create these four tags in ActiveCampaign → Tags:
SS-NEW SS-WARM SS-HOT SS-APPLIED
These will be applied automatically based on behaviour.
6
Replace the form on Page 1
Open page1-optin.html in a text editor. Find the <form id="optinForm"> section and replace it entirely with your ActiveCampaign embed code. Save and re-upload to Netlify.
03
// PHASE THREE — The Glue
Connect Everything With Zapier
~2 hours
⚡Zapier
zapier.com → Free tier (100 tasks/mo)
Zapier is the invisible glue. It watches for things happening in one tool and triggers actions in another — automatically, no code needed. You'll use it to route qualified applicants and apply lead scores.
// THE THREE ZAPS YOU NEED
1
Zap 1: Application → ActiveCampaign tag
Trigger: New Typeform submission
Action: Find contact in ActiveCampaign by email → Apply tag SS-APPLIED
This fires the moment someone submits the apply form.
2
Zap 2: Score HOT leads automatically
Trigger: New Typeform submission where answer to "investment readiness" = "Yes — I'm ready"
Action: Apply tag SS-HOT in ActiveCampaign → Send notification email to yourself
You get alerted the instant a HOT lead applies.
3
Zap 3: HOT lead → route to offer page email
Trigger: Tag SS-HOT added to contact
Action: Send email via ActiveCampaign with subject "Your application is approved — here's your offer" and link to page4-offer.html
This is the callless conversion trigger.
04
// PHASE FOUR — The Application
Set Up The Application Form
~1 hour
📋Typeform
typeform.com → Free tier available
Typeform replaces the built-in form on page3-apply.html with a properly connected form that sends data to Zapier and ActiveCampaign. High completion rate on mobile — critical for a funnel built for phones.
// SETUP STEPS
1
Create your form
New Typeform → build 7 questions matching exactly what's on page3-apply.html. Use the same questions — current situation, income goal, prior experience, investment readiness, why you want to join, anything else.
2
Style it to match
In Typeform design settings: background #05020e, accent colour #a855f7, font: any sans-serif. This keeps it on-brand.
3
Embed or link from page 3
Either embed the Typeform directly into page3-apply.html using the embed code, or simply update the form's submit button to redirect to your Typeform URL. The redirect is simpler and works well on mobile.
4
Connect to Zapier
In Typeform → Connect → Zapier. Authorise the connection. This is what Zap 1 and Zap 2 above will listen to.
05
// PHASE FIVE — The Chatbot
Add The AI Chatbot
~2 hours
🤖Tidio
tidio.com → $29/month
Tidio puts an AI chat widget on every page of your funnel. It handles objections, answers questions, and qualifies leads at 2am when you're asleep. The chatbot script we've built tells it exactly what to say — you just paste it in.
// SETUP STEPS
1
Create account and install widget
Sign up at tidio.com. In Settings → Channels → Live Chat → get your install code. It's a small JavaScript snippet.
2
Add the snippet to all pages
Paste the Tidio script just before the </body> tag in page1, page2, page3, and page4. Re-upload all four to Netlify.
3
Train your AI with the chatbot script
In Tidio → Lyro AI → Knowledge Base. Paste the full chatbot qualification script (see the chatbot script document). This teaches the AI your offer, your objections, and your qualifying questions.
4
Style to match brand
In Tidio → Appearance: set colour to #a855f7, widget position bottom-right, greeting message: "Hey — got questions about the system? I'm here."
